# Things only you can do

Lanes append click-by-click steps here when they hit something a machine cannot
do — creating an account, paying, using a physical device, or supplying a
credential (LANE step 12). A lane never waits on this file; it records the steps
and moves to another card.

Nothing is outstanding. Cloudflare Pages is already connected — production
branch `develop`, live at https://innana-seven-gates.pages.dev/
