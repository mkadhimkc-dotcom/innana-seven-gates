# gate-01-01 — The First Gate (critical path)

Gate I teaches one mechanic per level (SPEC 20). This level teaches keys and
doors (SPEC 14) and nothing else: no blocks, no torches, no hazards.

This doc is the template for every level's critical-path doc. All 52 questions
from SPEC 52 must be answered before a level enters Human QA; the answers below
cover the design questions. Questions 27-31 and 46-52 are filled in by the
validator run and the three playtests, and are marked `pending` until those
happen (SPEC 20, SPEC 48).
